# The Ash Archive — Story Guide

## Premise

Chief Archivist Ilyra Sorn disappears after discovering that accession cards 7A–7D have been removed from the Ash Archive. The missing catalog run points toward old river-evacuation records and an obsolete emergency courier network marked by an erased-wing heron.

Mara Vale, Ilyra’s protégé, is trying to investigate without surrendering the Archive to Councillor Caldrin Vey or Captain Tomas Rusk. Oren Thane arrives after closing with a sealed heron letter. The letter is the immediate narrative object; the catalog gap, vault, map tile, and relationships provide multiple routes into the mystery.

## Mystery layers

### Character-accessible to Mara

- Catalog cards are missing.
- Nessa holds Token 7B.
- Ilyra is missing.
- The vault is watch-sealed.
- The burned leaf bears an erased-wing heron and partial coordinate.
- The heron marked an emergency courier network.
- Caldrin requested the ledger and later returned to the South Court.
- Oren deliberately gave Mara the sealed letter.
- In the active scene, the wax is warm and Oren’s cuff has fresh river mud.

### Known by other characters but not Mara

- Oren knows Ilyra’s contingency instruction and Sable’s role.
- Sable knows the map tile’s loss and the unseen method by which she received the letter.
- Edrin suspects Ilyra reactivated the courier network.
- Rusk suspects internal theft and holds the map tile.
- Caldrin believes the catalog gap protects river records.

### Author-only at tick 121

- Ilyra is alive in a concealed undercroft.
- The letter names Token 7B, the north shelf, and the warning against an intact heron seal.
- The map tile reveals a drainage route into the undercroft.
- Nessa is approaching the Reading Room but has not entered.

## Current choice space

The active scene supports several legitimate continuations:

- Mara questions Oren before touching the seal.
- Mara hides the letter when Nessa enters.
- Mara breaks the seal.
- Mara compares the heron seal with Caldrin’s signet.
- Oren reveals or withholds Sable’s role.
- The soft catalog footstep becomes an interruption.

None is canon until a new event/change set is committed.
