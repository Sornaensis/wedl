# Completed Ash Archive Walkthrough

The bundled Ash Archive is now a complete mystery rather than an indefinitely active demonstration world.

## Movement I — The gap

Ilyra assigns Mara an audit of accession cards. Nessa finds cards 7A–7D missing and Token 7B lodged behind the rail. Councillor Caldrin requests a restricted ember ledger; Ilyra refuses him. Ilyra then disappears after entering the Restricted Vault.

Mara finds a burned ledger leaf marked with an erased-wing heron. Brother Edrin identifies the mark as an emergency courier sign. Oren arrives after closing with a warm-sealed letter bearing the same mark.

## Movement II — The route

Nessa's interruption prevents Mara from opening the letter immediately. Oren reveals that Token 7B releases a hidden rail beneath the seventh drawer. Mara and Nessa recover a retired counterseal, a repair slip, and a listening tube.

Notary Ysabet Crane serves a Council inventory writ. The broken counterseal proves the writ was sealed with a retired matrix, and Ysabet creates one hour of procedural space.

The repair loft's bell tube identifies North Flood Gallery N-7B. Beneath the Archive, Mara and Nessa recover a flood register and Ilyra's graphite ribbon. The records implicate Captain Rusk's requisition cipher but leave open the possibility of planted evidence.

The group escapes through Pressure Gate Seven, opens Ilyra's letter under witness, enters the Council listening office, and establishes a surveillance chain linking Council procedure, Archive removals, and the old courier route.

## Movement III — The record

The ledger hearing forces private evidence into public provenance. Rusk gives a qualified deposition. Caldrin attempts to disavow the surveillance system. Mara finds Ilyra alive beneath the vault; Ilyra explains that the descendant ledger protects real families while also making them vulnerable to any central custodian.

At `The Choice of Records`, Nessa proposes three partial records: proof, route, and identity. Mara turns the proposal into a temporary, expiring custody instrument:

- Ysabet holds proof under notarial seal.
- Sister Ansel holds the route key.
- Ilyra holds the unindexed identity packets pending further division.
- Five witnesses record the destruction of the reconstruction cross-index.

The alarm forces the custodians apart. At the River Gate, Rusk returns Sable's confiscated map and agrees to make a public statement rather than demand custody of the route. At Ember Hall, Caldrin loses Archive authority and Rusk loses Archive access while remaining a compelled witness.

## Epilogue — An honest absence

Mara, Ilyra, Nessa, and Oren establish the first `Ledger of Honest Absences`. It records that protected material existed, its scope, witnesses, reason for omission, expiration, and review date—but not the identities whose central reconstruction would recreate the original danger.

Ilyra returns the restricted-vault key to Mara under review. The Archive remains incomplete by design, and the incompleteness itself has provenance.
