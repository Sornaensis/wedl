# The Frontiersmen — World Guide

## Premise

**The Frontiersmen** follows five newly hired members of the Lantern Pike monster-hunting guild as they enter the remote peninsula called **the Frontier**. Their first assignment is ordinary caravan protection. It becomes village duty, then an investigation into apparitions that pass through walls and soil, then a two-day underground ordeal, and finally a flight from an amber-empowered cult in the unmapped northwood.

The example is intentionally an **open campaign**, not a completed novella. Its active scene is **Southward Cut** at `main 210:0`. The party has broken the immediate Root Host pursuit at a drowned waymark and is moving south along an abandoned warden road whose destination is uncertain.

| **Pip Fenlock** | Halfling locksmith, scout, and cartographer | Notices mechanisms, omissions, route logic, and the ways maps cease to be honest |

The party is deliberately complementary without being a direct transcription of D&D classes. The setting supports familiar fantasy-adventure play while keeping wedl's records concerned with **what happened, who perceived it, and what each person believes**.

## The Frontier

The Frontier is a cold peninsula of boreal forest, exposed coasts, boggy lowlands, and scattered pockets of farmable soil. The Keld River enters the sea at **Keldmouth**, the only true port and administrative center. East of Keldmouth, settlements cling to a semi-coastal road because the interior forest is difficult to clear, easy to become lost in, and increasingly dangerous around amber workings.

### Amber

Frontier amber is not fossil resin. It looks like liquid gold held just short of flowing. Near-surface pockets are located by gnome dowsers whose forked rods answer to concentrations in soil and stone. Amber functions as:

- currency;
- guild payment;
- mine output;
- a sympathetic magical material;
- an unacknowledged cause or amplifier of manifestations.

Ordinary newcomers are not given a useful explanation. They are shown how to weigh it, told where it spends, and discouraged from asking why the guild accepts it more readily than coined money.

### Author truth

Amber is hardened memory-sap associated with the buried **Heartwood Below**. Blood, fear, violent fracture, and concentrated remembered suffering can quicken it. Under those conditions it may produce ethereal **wretches**: animal-like or hybrid revenants built from remembered predator, prey, injury, and pursuit. The Lantern Pike discovered this correlation at Blackroot Sink and concealed it to preserve its amber-funded protection system.

Secret-marker regression token:

```text
FRONTIER-SECRET-AMBER-ROOT-MEMORY-7K4M
```

Character search must never expose that token.

## Major locations

### Keldmouth

A river-mouth port built from wet timber, stone embankments, cranes, fish markets, guild halls, and amber exchange houses. New hires first experience the Frontier as an administrative economy: contracts, badges, weighed amber, and evasive answers.

### Harrowcross

A crossroads town surrounded by low farms and an east orchard. The local watch has been slaughtered overnight. Sergeant Edrik Morn has one surviving man, Kellan Voss, and no language for what came through the barracks wall. Harrowcross pays for Lantern Pike protection with amber collected from farmers and nearby workings.

### Saint Orra's Amber Mine

A mine east of Harrowcross. Foreman Jorund Bale has sealed a warm drift after a worker disappeared and a living amber seam began answering sound, blood, and proximity. The party enters despite his reluctance, fights a mine wretch, and is cut off by a cave-in.

### Lower Caverns and Sunken Hall

The party spends two days underground. They cross black water, find an old camp, and enter ruins older than the present settlements. A tablet describes amber as golden sap through which root remembers wound. A reliquary and shifting gallery release remembered predators before the party finds a surface shaft.

### Root-Crowned Camp

A northwood encampment ruled by the self-styled **Tree King**, once Lantern Pike field-master Aldren Veyl. His followers wear frightening wooden masks and do not speak. He claims communion with the Frontier and uses amber, fear, blood sport, and masks to strengthen a collective host.

## Conversation provenance

The example contains 19 conversations, 248 canonical turns, and 76 recollections. Important examples include:

- **The Last Watch** — reconstructs the guard catastrophe without granting the party perfect knowledge before testimony.
- **Kellan's Broken Watch** — shows how trauma, remembered voices, and incomplete observation differ from the canonical event.
- **After the Cave-In** — captures immediate tactical decisions while the mine remains unstable.
- **Second Night Below** — lets each party member interpret the cavern's response to memory differently.
- **The Sunken Hall Debate** — preserves exact disagreement over whether to open the reliquary.
- **The Tree King's Offer** — records Aldren's doctrine and Rootjaw's nonverbal warning while Moth remains mute.
- **Before the Blood Ring** — records the party's planning and Moth's silent intervention.
- **Running Under the Drums** — separates distant audibility from physical scene presence.
- **Water Over the Mark** — closes the immediate pursuit while keeping the reliquary shut and the key's hooked notch unused.
- **The Southward Cut** — inventories one narrow piece of evidence and one unconfirmed route without manufacturing certainty.

```bash
wedl init frontiersmen --example frontiersmen
cd frontiersmen
wedl validate
wedl compile --profile hybrid --vector-provider lsa
```

Build one of the final viewpoint packets:

```bash
wedl context "Rhea" \
  --scene "Southward Cut" \
  --query "What does the counterfoil prove, and what does the southward road promise?" \
  --max-characters 5000
```

Compare author truth and character scope:

```bash
wedl context "Veyra" --scene "Southward Cut" --perspective author
wedl context "Veyra" --scene "Southward Cut" --perspective dramatic-irony
```

Inspect the exact Tree King transcript:

```bash
wedl conversation show "The Tree King's Offer"
```

Inspect Rootjaw's limited audible portion of the pursuit:

```bash
wedl conversation show "Running Under the Drums" \
  --perspective character \
  --character "Rootjaw"
```

Run independent retrieval lanes:

```bash
wedl search "blood quickens amber" --mode fts
wedl search "memory of wounded animals taking shape" --mode vector
wedl search "guild concealed amber manifestations" --mode hybrid
```
