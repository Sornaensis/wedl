# Reading event consequences

Open an event in the lore reader with the author lens selected. Choose an author horizon to read through that story moment. Full story uses the terminal supported boundary of the selected timeline; it does not invent a current time or combine timelines. Character views do not request the author consequence report.

The article keeps its prose and direct authored effects. Recorded consequences distinguish changes around the event, transitions explicitly naming it as their cause, outcome links, later causal events, and the contribution remaining at the chosen horizon. Earlier accepted beliefs remain in their recorded history when later authored evidence rejects them. The current contribution can say that it was superseded by later authored evidence.

Named links open the recorded lore entry. Empty sections mean no links were recorded in that report; they do not prove that the story is complete or verified. This reader does not submit expectation checks or write consequences.

Loading and failure messages are announced through a live status region. An unavailable, invalid, or bounded report leaves the article and direct effects readable. Reopen the entry or choose a reading horizon to try again after the source or connection becomes available. Report requests remain pinned to their captured revision and horizon; superseded responses are ignored. Browser Back and Forward retain the reading context.

## Source detail enrichment

The report endpoint uses the local author session. Optional labels for a report-admitted knowledge or relationship record use the existing public source-detail endpoint. That endpoint is a public read even when a session token is sent. Its matching source blob proves content identity; the authenticated report supplies admission.

The reader accepts only the admitted record identity, kind and source blob at the captured revision, rejects conflicting context metadata, and copies only the declared knower, claim statement, and relationship endpoints. It reads at most 24 distinct admitted records with four requests in flight. Missing proof or failed detail reads retain the report's recorded name. No source scan or inference from titles supplies a direction.

Automatic revision refresh requires the server runtime's WebSocket support. An explicit page refresh reloads the current source when that channel is unavailable.
