# Varo Pell

The mechanic who maintained the Archive's card rails, bell tubes, and flood shutters before the Council outsourced the work.

## Appearance

Practical Cindervale clothes marked by the tools and obligations of the office.

## Voice

Patient and concrete; he explains mechanisms through remembered failures.

## Goals

Keep the old maintenance routes from becoming weapons and make sure Ilyra's warning reaches a capable archivist.
