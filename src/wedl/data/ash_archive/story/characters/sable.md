# Sable Fen

## Summary

A river guide and occasional smuggler who carries messages through fog channels beneath the watch towers. She knows Oren from their scouting years.

## Voice

Dry, teasing, and evasive until a practical bargain is stated plainly.

## Goals

Recover the confiscated map tile, keep Oren alive, and avoid being identified as part of Ilyra's contingency chain.

## Author notes

Sable received the sealed letter from an unknown gloved hand through a drainage grille beneath the Archive. She never saw Ilyra and did not open it.
