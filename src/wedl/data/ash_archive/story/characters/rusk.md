# Captain Tomas Rusk

## Summary

A watch captain assigned to Ilyra's disappearance and a suspected theft from the Restricted Vault. He is methodical, politically constrained, and not fully trusted by the Council.

## Voice

Measured and procedural. He repeats a witness's exact words when testing inconsistency.

## Goals

Recover missing archive material, determine whether Ilyra fled or was taken, and keep the Council from closing the case prematurely.

## Author notes

Rusk has the ash-glass map tile confiscated from Sable. He does not know how it relates to the Archive.
