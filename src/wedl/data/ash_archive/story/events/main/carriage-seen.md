# Nessa Sees Caldrin's Carriage

From the upper catalog window, Nessa sees Caldrin's unmarked carriage enter the service court after the watch has sealed the vault. She cannot see whether Caldrin enters the building.

Nessa later tells Mara, supporting—but not proving—Mara's suspicion.
