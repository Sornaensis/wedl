# Sealed Heron Letter

A folded sheet under thick gray wax. The seal bears a heron with its left wing deliberately erased. The wax is still warm when Mara touches it at tick 121.

## Author notes

The letter says that Ilyra is alive beneath the Restricted Vault, that Catalog Token 7B identifies the correct north-shelf ledger, and that Mara must not trust an intact heron seal.
