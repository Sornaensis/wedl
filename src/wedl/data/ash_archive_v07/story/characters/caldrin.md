# Councillor Caldrin Vey

## Summary

A senior councillor who funds the Archive's fireproofing and expects institutional gratitude in return. He requested a restricted ember ledger shortly before Ilyra disappeared.

## Voice

Warm, patient, and framed as shared civic concern. He rarely gives a direct order when a favor will create more obligation.

## Goals

Recover evidence connecting his family to the illegal river evacuations, identify who revived the erased-wing courier sign, and keep the watch focused on ordinary theft.

## Author notes

Caldrin owns the intact erased-wing signet. He did not write the sealed letter, though Mara may reasonably suspect him.
