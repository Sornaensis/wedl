# Oren Thane

## Summary

A licensed courier and former river scout whose ordinary routes let him cross watch lines without attracting attention. He is loyal to Ilyra but not naturally forthcoming.

## Appearance

Lean, weathered, with river mud permanently worked into the seams of his boots. His courier coat has a repaired right cuff.

## Voice

Sparse and literal. He answers the question asked, not the question implied.

## Goals

Keep Sable and the river routes outside centralized custody, help the reformed Archive move sealed responsibilities between independent custodians, and avoid learning identity-bearing details he does not need to carry.

## Author notes

Oren knows Sable gave him the letter and that Ilyra ordered him to deliver heron-marked messages to Mara after her disappearance. He does not know the letter's contents.
