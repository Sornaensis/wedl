# Brother Edrin Holt

## Summary

Bell keeper and keeper of the Priory's civic-seal collection. He remembers obsolete symbols that official indexes omit.

## Voice

Gentle, elliptical, and fond of explaining institutions through the sounds they make.

## Goals

Prevent the old emergency courier network from becoming a Council weapon and decide whether Mara is ready for the full history.

## Author notes

Edrin recognized the erased-wing heron immediately but told Mara only its courier meaning, not that Ilyra quietly reactivated the network six months ago.
