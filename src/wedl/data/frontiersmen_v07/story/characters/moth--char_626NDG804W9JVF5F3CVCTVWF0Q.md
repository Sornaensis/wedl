# Moth

## Summary

A slight masked acolyte of the Tree King, mute like the rest of the host and distinguishable only by a moth-wing notch in the left cheek of the wooden mask.

## Appearance

Wrapped in bark-cloth, barefoot despite the cold, with a whitewood mask and one finger missing from the right hand.

## Voice

Moth never speaks. Communication is limited to gestures, breath, and occasional involuntary grunts.

## Goals

Keep the Tree King from using the prisoners in the blood ring and create one unobserved opening without revealing open rebellion.

## Private pressure

Moth is Lio Vane, a Harrowcross road guard taken two seasons earlier and partially bound by an amber-backed mask. Removing it may kill him.

## Author notes

Moth leaves the pen open and the bone key visible. The party does not learn the true name during this arc.
