# Senna Reed

## Summary

A flax farmer whose orchard borders the old mine track. She saw an antlered shape pass through an apple-tree trunk without bending a branch.

## Appearance

Strong-backed, middle-aged, with an orchard knife, a green scarf, and frost-burned fingertips.

## Voice

Concrete rural speech. She distrusts explanations that make witnesses sound foolish.

## Goals

Protect her household, get the eastern orchard reopened, and make the guild admit the attacks follow amber carts.

## Private pressure

Senna's husband secretly accepted raw amber instead of coin and buried it beneath their hearth.

## Author notes

Her testimony points toward movement through matter and the coastal mine road.
