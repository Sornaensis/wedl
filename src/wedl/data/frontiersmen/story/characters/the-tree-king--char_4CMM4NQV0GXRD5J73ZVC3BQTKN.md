# The Tree King

## Summary

A self-crowned sovereign of a masked woodland host who claims communion with the Frontier and demonstrates terrifying control over quickened amber.

## Appearance

Tall and gaunt beneath a crown of living roots pinned through an old guild officer's hood. Amber veins shine under scar tissue along his throat.

## Voice

Courtly, expansive, and convinced that cruelty becomes natural law when named a trial. He speaks to the forest as if it answers between words.

## Goals

Strengthen his host through blood sport, break the guild's monopoly on amber, and force Veyra to admit that the substance chooses worthy vessels.

## Private pressure

He was Aldren Veyl, Halric's field-master at Blackroot. Exposure to a blooded seam let him hear the Heartwood's stored lives; he mistakes overwhelming memory for kingship.

## Author notes

He can provoke manifestations by feeding blood and fear into carved amber lattices. His communion is real, but his interpretation is self-serving.
